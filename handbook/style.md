# Style

## Who we write for
The owner in `gazette.config.json`: a busy builder who wants each read to **teach something or spark something to try the same day**. Every edition does at least one of those.

## Rules
- **Plain language.** Explain the mechanism, not the hype. If a term needs the glossary, add it to the glossary.
- **Punch line first.** Front cards must make sense on a phone in two lines.
- **The paper's voice, not paste.** Rewrite. Quote sparingly. Primary sources go on the story page under Receipts.
- **Voice:** dry, sharp, respectful of builders. Short sentences where they hit.
- **Clean headlines.** No internal status words. Labels that matter to the reader (RUMOR, vendor numbers) stay visible.
- **Surface depth over headline depth.** Cover the API, tools, limits and pricing, not just one number.
- **Click-worthy bar.** Teaches a mechanism or tradeoff, OR sparks a same-day prototype, OR changes a cost, architecture or watchlist choice. Otherwise it goes on the ticker.
- **Newest and highest-signal on the front.** Cool older stories to the archive. Never delete.
- **One-liners stay thin.** They go on the ticker or the wire, not dressed up as stories.

## Story shape
1. Kicker (section · status · date), headline, dek, byline, chips, Save
2. **What happened**: exactly two lines
3. **Why it matters**: one or two sentences
4. **Details**: short paragraphs (≤ ~70 words), then *What to learn / try*
5. The paper's take, in its own box, not as an extra heading
6. **Receipts**: primary links with fact status and checked time
7. **Related**: chain prev/next, explainers and build ideas, related news, section, threads, glossary

## Explainers
Built from zero: the one-line idea → how it works → what it does *not* do → a worked example → a self-test. Label vendor numbers. Say pass@k or pass^k. Project-specific takeaways go in `for_you` (private).
