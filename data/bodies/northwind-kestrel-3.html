The price is the headline. The open Mini is the part that changes how you build.

The two models

Kestrel 3 is Northwind's new flagship. It reads up to 400K tokens in one call and costs $3 per million tokens in and $15 out — a fifth of Kestrel 2. Northwind's own benchmark table puts it ahead of Kestrel 2 on every task it lists; nobody outside Northwind has run those tests yet.

Kestrel 3 Mini is the small one: open weights, a permissive licence, and Northwind says it runs on a 32 GB laptop. Over the API it costs $0.20 in / $0.80 out.

What is still unknown

  • No independent benchmark run yet (watchlist).
  • No batch pricing on the page.
  • Mini's real memory use on a laptop is Northwind's claim, not a measurement.
