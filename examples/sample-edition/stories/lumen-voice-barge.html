Lumen Voice lets you interrupt its phone agent mid-sentence — and it stops talking in under 200 ms

Lumen Voice, seed-stage startup · full-duplex calls · the number is the company's own

By Informer · Vol. 1, No. 3

StartupsVoiceAgents

What happened

Lumen Voice launched a phone agent that keeps listening while it speaks and stops when the caller talks over it.

It says the agent stops in under 200 ms on average; no outside test yet.

Why it matters

How fast an agent yields when you interrupt is what makes a voice agent feel like a person or like a menu.
