Northwind’s Kestrel 3 costs a fifth of Kestrel 2, reads 400K tokens at once — and its Mini version runs on a laptop

Northwind Labs, Mon 2 Nov · two models, one API · price cut, longer context, a small model with open weights

By Informer · Vol. 1, No. 3

ModelsPricingAgentsOpen weights

What happened

Northwind Labs released Kestrel 3 at $3 in / $15 out per million tokens, down from $15/$75 for Kestrel 2, with a 400K-token context window.

Kestrel 3 Mini ships with open weights under a permissive licence and runs on a 32 GB laptop, Northwind says.

Why it matters

A fivefold price cut changes which jobs are worth handing to a big model; the open Mini makes a cheap local first pass realistic.

Details

The price is the headline. The open Mini is the part that changes how you build.

The two models

Kestrel 3 is Northwind's new flagship. It reads up to 400K tokens in one call and costs $3 per million tokens in and $15 out — a fifth of Kestrel 2. Northwind's own benchmark table puts it ahead of Kestrel 2 on every task it lists; nobody outside Northwind has run those tests yet.

Kestrel 3 Mini is the small one: open weights, a permissive licence, and Northwind says it runs on a 32 GB laptop. Over the API it costs $0.20 in / $0.80 out.

What is still unknown

  • No independent benchmark run yet (watchlist).
  • No batch pricing on the page.
  • Mini's real memory use on a laptop is Northwind's claim, not a measurement.

For Pocketful: the Mini is the candidate for on-device categorising — see today's Build idea.

Informer’s take

Price is the headline, but the Mini is the story: a lab shipping a small open model next to its flagship invites a draft-locally, check-in-the-cloud pattern.

What to learn / try

  • Rerun your three most expensive prompts on Kestrel 3 and compare cost per correct answer, not cost per token.
  • Run Kestrel 3 Mini locally on a 20-item sample of your own data before believing any benchmark.
