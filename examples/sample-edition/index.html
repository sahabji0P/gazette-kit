Northwind’s Kestrel 3 costs a fifth of Kestrel 2, reads 400K tokens at once — and its Mini version runs on a laptop

What happened
Northwind Labs released Kestrel 3 at $3 in / $15 out per million tokens, down from $15/$75 for Kestrel 2, with a 400K-token context window. Kestrel 3 Mini ships with open weights under a permissive licence and runs on a 32 GB laptop, Northwind says.
Why it matters
A fivefold price cut changes which jobs are worth handing to a big model; the open Mini makes a cheap local first pass realistic.
