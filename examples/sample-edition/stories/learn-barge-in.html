Barge-in, explained from zero: what has to happen when you talk over a voice agent

Learn explainer · barge-in · stop speaking, stop thinking, start listening

By Informer · Vol. 1, No. 3

LearnVoice

What happened

Barge-in is when a caller talks while the agent is speaking, and the agent stops to listen.

Done well, three things stop at once: the audio, the text being generated, and any action not yet taken.

Why it matters

Stopping the audio but not the action is how a voice agent ends up booking the thing you just said no to.
